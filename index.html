OUR STORY, IN YOUR LANGUAGE

ENGLISH

FAKA LAPI is rooted in the language, creativity and everyday culture of our people. The expression is used in villages and townships alike. Born from a community spirit that crosses neighbourhoods and boundaries, our streetwear is for everyone who takes pride in where they come from and how they show up. One culture. One style. FAKA LAPI.

ISIZULU

I-FAKA LAPI isekelwe olimini, ebucikweni nasemasikweni ansuku zonke abantu bakithi. Lesi sisho sisetshenziswa emakhaya nasemalokishini ngokufanayo. Iqhamuka emoyeni wobumbano lomphakathi oweqa imingcele yezindawo nomakhelwane, imfashini yethu yasemgwaqweni ingeyawo wonke umuntu oziqhenyayo ngemvelaphi yakhe nangendlela aziveza ngayo. Isiko elilodwa. Isitayela esisodwa. FAKA LAPI.

ISIXHOSA

I-FAKA LAPI isekelwe kulwimi, kubuchule nakwinkcubeko yemihla ngemihla yabantu bethu. Eli binzana lisetyenziswa ezilalini nasezilokishini ngokufanayo. Iphuma kumoya womanyano loluntu owela imida yeendawo nabamelwane, impahla yethu yesitrato yeyomntu wonke ozidlayo ngemvelaphi yakhe nangendlela azibonakalisa ngayo. Inkcubeko enye. Isimbo esinye. FAKA LAPI.

AFRIKAANS

FAKA LAPI is gewortel in die taal, kreatiwiteit en alledaagse kultuur van ons mense. Die uitdrukking word in dorpe en townships gebruik. Dit spruit uit ’n gemeenskapsgees wat woonbuurte en grense oorbrug. Ons straatklere is vir almal wat trots is op waar hulle vandaan kom en hoe hulle hulself uitleef. Een kultuur. Een styl. FAKA LAPI.

SEPEDI

FAKA LAPI e theilwe polelong, boitlhamelong le setšong sa letšatši le letšatši sa batho ba rena. Polelwana ye e dirišwa metseng ya magaeng le makeišene ka go swana. E tšwa moyeng wa botee bja setšhaba wo o kopanyago mafelo le mellwane. Diaparo tša rena tša mebileng ke tša motho yo mongwe le yo mongwe yo a ikgantšhago ka moo a tšwago gona le ka tsela ye a itšweletšago ka yona. Setšo se tee. Setaele se tee. FAKA LAPI.

SESOTHO

FAKA LAPI e thehiloe puong, boqaping le moetlong oa letsatsi le letsatsi oa batho ba rona. Polelo ena e sebelisoa metseng ea mahaeng le makeisheneng ka ho tšoana. E tsoetsoe ke moea oa bonngoe ba sechaba o kopanyang libaka le meeli. Liaparo tsa rona tsa seterateng ke tsa bohle ba motlotlo ka moo ba tsoang teng le kamoo ba itlhahisang kateng. Setso se le seng. Setaele se le seng. FAKA LAPI.

SETSWANA

FAKA LAPI e theilwe mo puong, boitlhamelong le mo setsong sa letsatsi le letsatsi sa batho ba rona. Polelwana eno e dirisiwa kwa metseng ya magae le kwa ditoropong ka go tshwana. E tsaletswe ke moya wa kutlwano wa setšhaba o o kopanyang mafelo le melelwane. Diaparo tsa rona tsa mo mebileng ke tsa mongwe le mongwe yo o ipelang ka kwa a tswang teng le ka tsela e a itlhalosang ka yone. Setso se le sengwe. Setaele se le sengwe. FAKA LAPI.

SISWATI

I-FAKA LAPI igcile elulwimini, ekucambeni nasekwenteni kwansuku tonkhe kwebantfu bakitsi. Lesisho sisetjentiswa emakhaya nasemalokishini ngalokulinganako. Italwe ngumoya webunye bemphakatsi loweqa imingcele yetindzawo, timphahla tetfu temgwaco tentelwe wonkhe umuntfu lotigcabha ngekutsi uvelaphi nangendlela latetfula ngayo. Lisiko linye. Sitayela sinye. FAKA LAPI.

TSHIVENDA

FAKA LAPI yo thewa nga luambo, vhutsila na mvelele ya vhutshilo ha ḓuvha ḽiṅwe na ḽiṅwe ya vhathu vhashu. Mubulo uyu u shumiswa mahayani na dzidoroboni nga u eḓana. Yo bebwa nga muya wa vhuthihi ha tshitshavha une wa pfukisa mikano ya fhethu na vhahura. Zwiambaro zwashu zwa mitaani ndi zwa muṅwe na muṅwe ane a ḓihudza nga he a bva hone na nḓila ine a ḓi sumbedza ngayo. Mvelele nthihi. Tshitaela tshithihi. FAKA LAPI.

XITSONGA

FAKA LAPI yi huma eka ririmi, vutumbuluxi ni mahanyelo ya siku na siku ya vanhu va hina. Xiga lexi xi tirhisiwa ematikoxikaya ni le madorobeni. Yi tumbuluxiwile hi moya wa vun’we wa vaaki lowu hlanganisaka tindhawu ni mindzilekana. Swiambalo swa hina swa xitalo i swa un’wana ni un’wana loyi a tinyungubyisaka hi laha a humaka kona ni ndlela leyi a tivonisaka ha yona. Ndhavuko wun’we. Xitayili xin’we. FAKA LAPI.

ISINDEBELE

I-FAKA LAPI isekelwe elimini, ekusunguleni izinto ezintsha namasikweni wamalanga woke wabantu bekhethu. Isitjho lesi sisetjenziswa emakhaya nasemalokitjhini ngokufanako. Sivela emoyeni wobunye bomphakathi oweqa imikhawulo yeendawo nabomakhelwana. Izambatho zethu zendlela zenzelwe woke umuntu ozikhakhazisa ngalapho avela khona nangendlela aziveza ngayo. Isiko linye. Isitayela sinye. FAKA LAPI.
