FAKA LAPI is rooted in the language, creativity and everyday culture of our people. The expression is used in villages and townships alike. Born from a community spirit that crosses neighbourhoods and boundaries, our streetwear is for everyone who takes pride in where they come from and how they show up. One culture. One style.
